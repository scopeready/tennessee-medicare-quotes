Who We Are

ECOS Medicare Solutions (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is an independent insurance agency operated by Darin Weidauer (NPN #18580338). Our website is www.tennesseemedicarequotes.com. We specialize in Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement (Medigap), and Medicare Part D plans in Tennessee.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out a form on our website, call us, or download a resource. This may include:

Your name, email address, phone number, zip code, and Medicare plan interest. We do not collect Social Security numbers, bank account information, credit card numbers, or health records through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to contact you about Medicare plan options, provide personalized quotes, assist with enrollment, and send educational Medicare resources you've requested. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

How We Collect Information

We use Web3Forms to process form submissions. When you submit a form, your information is sent securely to our email. We may also use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use our website. This tool collects anonymous usage data such as pages visited, time on site, and general location — it does not collect your name, email, or phone number unless you submit a form.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies from Google Analytics for website analytics purposes. These cookies help us understand site usage patterns. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Our website does not use cookies for advertising or retargeting.

Your Consent to Be Contacted

By submitting a form on our website, you provide your express written consent for ECOS Medicare Solutions and its licensed agents to contact you at the phone number and/or email address provided, regarding Medicare plan options. This may include calls, text messages (SMS), and emails. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message, replying to any email with “unsubscribe,” or calling us at (702) 706-6564.

TCPA Disclosure

By providing your phone number and checking the consent box on our forms, you consent to receive calls and/or text messages from ECOS Medicare Solutions at the number provided. Calls may be made using an automatic telephone dialing system or prerecorded voice. Your consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services. Message and data rates may apply. You may revoke consent at any time.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including SSL/TLS encryption on our website and secure email delivery. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (such as Medicare.gov, Social Security Administration, or insurance carrier websites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
