Medicare Advantage and Part D plan availability is set county by county and changes every plan year. Two people 30 miles apart in different counties can see completely different menus of plans, premiums and networks. It is the single biggest factor in what you can actually choose.
Both. Your county of residence determines which plans you are eligible to join. The network where you actually get care determines whether a plan is usable. This comes up constantly around Nashville, Memphis and the Tri-Cities, where county lines and metro commuting patterns don't line up.
