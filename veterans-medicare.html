What Every Veteran Needs to Know About Medicare

Yes. TRICARE For Life (TFL) requires you to have Medicare Part A and Part B. TFL acts as a supplement to Medicare — Medicare pays first, then TFL covers most of what's left. If you don't enroll in Part B, you lose your TFL coverage. This is one of the most common mistakes veterans make.
VA healthcare is not considered creditable coverage for Medicare Part B purposes. If you only use the VA and skip Part B, you may face permanent late enrollment penalties if you later decide to enroll. Many veterans enroll in Part B as a safety net — giving them access to any doctor, not just VA facilities, without penalty risk.

It depends on your situation. If you have TRICARE For Life, you may not need either — TFL already fills most Medicare gaps. If you rely primarily on VA healthcare, a low-cost Medicare Advantage plan could give you additional options outside the VA. Each situation is different, which is exactly why a free consultation with someone who understands both military benefits and Medicare is so valuable.
Some veterans qualify for Medicare's Extra Help (Low-Income Subsidy) program, which reduces Part D prescription drug costs. Your VA disability rating and income level affect eligibility. We can help determine if you qualify and assist with the application.
