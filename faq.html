Your Initial Enrollment Period (IEP) is a 7-month window starting 3 months before you turn 65. The Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) runs Oct 15 – Dec 7. The MA Open Enrollment runs Jan 1 – Mar 31. Special Enrollment Periods apply for qualifying life events.
Medicare Advantage replaces Original Medicare with an all-in-one plan. Medicare Supplement (Medigap) works alongside Original Medicare to cover out-of-pocket costs with freedom to see any Medicare-accepting doctor nationwide.
